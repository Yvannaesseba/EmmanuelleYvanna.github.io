About
A bit about me

I'm someone who genuinely finds data interesting, not just as a career path, but as a way of making sense of things. I like working on problems where the answer isn't obvious and where getting it wrong actually matters, which is probably why I've ended up drawn to healthcare and financial services.

I care a lot about building things that are fair and explainable, not just things that perform well on a metric. That question of whether a model can be trusted, not just whether it's accurate, is something I keep coming back to.

Outside of data I work with young people in Sheffield schools, which has taught me more about communicating clearly than any course has. I'm bilingual in English and French, and I'm always looking for roles where I can do work that means something.
